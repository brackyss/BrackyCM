Logistics Capabilities & Fleet

Commercial transport solutions equipped for diverse supply chain demands.

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated 53' dry van trailers engineered for maximum cubic capacity and direct regional or coast-to-coast delivery.

  • Up to 45,000 lbs max weight
  • 24/7 Satellite location tracking
  • High-value cargo seal integrity

Refrigerated Transport

Temperature-monitored reefers maintaining exact climate specifications for perishables, food product, and pharma.

  • -20°F to +70°F climate control
  • Continuous sensor temp logs
  • FSMA Sanitary certified

Flatbed & Step Deck

Specialized open decks tailored for industrial machinery, construction materials, and side-loaded freight.

  • Step decks & extendables
  • Permit & escort handling
  • Heavy strapping & tarps

Less Than Truckload (LTL)

Cost-effective palletized cargo consolidation for smaller shipments needing fast regional cross-dock distribution.

  • Cross-dock terminal network
  • Liftgate pickup capability
  • Consolidated tariff savings

Dedicated Dispatch Management

Contracted fleet management with dedicated dispatchers and account managers aligned with your supply chain.

  • Single point of contact
  • Guaranteed lane capacity
  • KPI performance reports

Expedited Hot Shot

  • Dual driver team capacity
